Send to Megatron

Paste a stream link, tap Send, then press one button on your TV.

Pairing

On the TV open Settings, Cast from another device. The code is shown there. You only enter it once.

Link

After sending, press Get URL from device on the TV to play it.

Queue

The TV picks up one link at a time, the newest one. Send them one by one.

Recently sent

Keep your pairing code private. Anyone who has it can send links to your TV. Do not post it or share screenshots of it. You can make a new code in the TV app's Settings.
