The media player your Samsung TV was missing

Play videos from a USB drive, SMB, WebDAV and DLNA. Browse like Windows Explorer, with watched percentages, fan art, subtitles in Arabic and more. Free and open source.

Samsung Tizen TVs · MIT license

What you get

01Explorer-style foldersFive views, sort by name, date, size or type. Big USB drives stay easy.
02Watched percentageA bar and a % on every video. You always land where you stopped.
03Better subtitlesAuto language, delay, an encoding picker for Arabic, saved looks.
04Your lookTen themes, twelve colours, Aurora or your own picture as background.
05Send from your phonePaste a link on your phone, press one button on the TV.
06You decide the networkAllow everything, only your servers, or nothing at all.

Install on your TV

  1. Turn on Developer Mode on the TV and install Apps2Samsung.
  2. In Apps2Samsung open Settings, Installing, Your sources and add https://github.com/Megatro-nn/megatron-tizen-tv.
  3. Install Megatron from the list. It also lists in the main catalog once it is accepted there.
Send a link to your TVPaste a stream link on your phone and pull it on the TV with one button. No account.
